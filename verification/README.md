# Reproduce the finite checks

From the repository root:

```bash
python -m pip install -r verification/requirements.txt
python verification/run_all.py
```

The script overwrites `verification/results.json` with the actual environment,
timestamp, source-script SHA-256, parameters, numerical observations and limits.
It uses only NumPy and SciPy, a fixed random seed, and local computation.

The recorded run passed seven groups in approximately 1.7 seconds with Python
3.12.14, NumPy 2.3.5 and SciPy 1.17.0. Its maximum discrepancy between the
independently constructed dense-state trace norm and the Jordan-sum bias was
8.9e-16. Runtime and the last few floating-point digits may vary by environment.

## What is checked

| Check | Independent construction | Tested scope |
|---|---|---|
| Global risk and prior activation | Gauss–Legendre/phase quadrature of actual Haar pure-product kets; dense eigenvalues of the averaged weighted difference; direct partial transpose of the optimal minority effect | n=1,...,4; nine priors including balance, endpoints and unequal interior priors |
| One-copy classical phase diagram | Finite physical-seed linear program using posterior states from the dense integrals; separate dense dual inequalities; actual complete POVMs generated by the 24 single-qubit Clifford rotations | Eight priors spanning all branches and both transition points; at most 48 attaining training outcomes |
| Finite classical plateau | Product-spin matrices and numerical total-spin eigenspaces; direct positive-semidefinite checks in every magnetic block; covariant seed normalization, twirl weights and payoff | n=1,...,8, at s=0,s0/2,s0; dense-integral payoff cross-checks for n<=4 |
| Rare-class threshold | Posterior eigenvalues of actual training effects; an extremal collective-training outcome saturating the boundary; a two-outcome POVM giving strict improvement above it | n=1,...,4; 70 random rank-one effects per n and extremal effects |
| Rare-class resource comparison | Dicke-isometry construction of the nonsymmetric comparison effect; direct false-positive and recall probabilities; quadrature of the covariant estimation protocol | n=1,...,4, plus integer sample-cost bounds at q=0.1,0.01,0.001,0.0001 |
| Several copies of one query | Haar-overlap quadrature for the all-orthogonal covariant test, symmetric-space rank arithmetic for retained comparison, and the worked-example target check | Five (dimension, reference copies, query copies) choices, including the two-query-qubit example |
| Finite noise and degenerate limits | Independently averaged depolarized three-qubit product states; trace distances and optimal global risk; a feasible classical POVM under noise; no-training direct state comparison | n=1,p=3/5,eta=0.01; n=0 at five priors |
| Fixed-prior asymptotic algebra | Complex pure-pair tangent-response vectors versus scalar V,C identities; independent angular quadrature of H; large-n exact global-sum convergence; direct integration of the independent covariant-estimation upper strategy | 600 random pure pairs, six fixed interior biases, global sums through n=64000, and a 1000-point coefficient-compatibility grid |

The main absolute matrix tolerance is 3e-10. The finite-grid primal LP is allowed
an approximation error below 3e-6 in bias. The corresponding analytical dual and
finite Clifford POVM are checked separately at the tighter matrix tolerance.
Recomputing the Haar integrals with a finer sphere rule is also checked.

The noisy example yields a guaranteed gap exceeding 0.0525937. Known
depolarization is simulable by either learner, so optimized noisy risks cannot
be lower than their noiseless optima. Combining classical monotonicity with
quantum continuity gives the pure-state gap minus
delta=1-(1-eta/2)^(2n+1). The suite computes the noisy global optimum but does not
optimize every noisy classical classifier. At the rare-class threshold equality, posterior ties
are explicitly retained: zero minority recall is forced only under strict
q<1/(n+2).

## What these checks do not establish

The asymptotic checks concern the scalar identities and integrals in
[the fixed-prior account](../science/asymptotic.md). They do not verify the
Bayesian Holevo theorem, its regularity assumptions, the patch-reassembly
argument or the augmentation used for the singular target. In particular they
do not establish that the displayed lower coefficient is an exact leading
classical coefficient.

These are finite floating-point calculations, not universal proofs, interval
certificates or an external review. They do not establish the maximal plateau,
the gap's asymptotic size at fixed prevalence, a fixed-noise large-n advantage,
reusable quantum inference, gate complexity or a hardware crossover. The
finite-seed LP is a feasible measurement optimization; it is not an exhaustive
search over the continuum by itself. The proofs and their assumptions are in
the scientific account. Passing calculations do not replace them.

The global Jordan formula and balanced classical construction are inherited
programmable-discrimination results. The universal comparison effect is also an
established primitive. Their independent numerical reconstruction checks the
conventions and their use in this contribution; it does not claim those
ingredients as new.
